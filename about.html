About

A family business built on quality and follow-through

Alvarado’s Custom & Countertops started with countertops and grew into residential, commercial, and multifamily work across Utah.

From countertops to complete installs

We began focused on countertop fabrication and installation. As clients asked for more — cabinets, remodels, commercial volume — we built the crew and processes to deliver.

Today we serve homeowners, contractors, and property managers with a bilingual team of 6–10, three years of hands-on experience, and a reputation for showing up and finishing clean.

  • Quality — careful edges, accurate fits, finishes that hold up.
  • Reliability — schedules respected and communication kept clear.
  • Production capacity — sized for multifamily and commercial volume.
  • Attention to detail — specialty stone work done with patience.
  • Problem-solving — field issues handled before they become delays.
  • Dependable completion — punch lists closed and jobs handed off right.
